# Workout Plan

**Goals:** general fitness, weight loss, body composition, and undoing the effects of a desk job (stiff back and hips).
**Structure:** two sessions a day, Sunday–Friday (a morning gym+pool session and a floor session at home with your son). Saturday is a short stretch and a family walk.
**In the app:** this plan is loaded from `public/data/` and is what the app shows each day. Sunday has **Push A + Easy Run** and **Floor: Mobility + Core**, and so on.

## Lifting (≈40 min)

**How to read these tables:**
- **Reps as a range, e.g. 8–10:** once you reach the top of the range on every set, add weight the next week. The app shows a hint when you're ready.
- **Superset (SS):** do one set of the first exercise, go straight to the second, then rest. The app moves you between the two automatically and only starts the rest timer at the end of each round.
- **First two weeks:** stop each set with 2–3 reps still in the tank.

**Starting weights** (kg) are starting points. Several are estimates, so adjust them in week 1. Values marked * came from your old plan.

## Cardio (30 min)

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| **Sun: Easy run** | 30 min at a pace where you can still talk (your current 5K pace or slower) |
| **Mon: Run + rope** | 10 min easy run · rope skipping **6 × 30s on / 30s off** · 10 min easy run |
| **Tue: Swim, steady** | 5 min easy · 20 min continuous freestyle at a moderate pace · 5 min easy |
| **Wed: Swim, technique** | 5 min easy · 20 min drills (4×50 m catch-up, 4×50 m fingertip drag, 4×50 m kickboard) · 5 min easy. Easy effort, because your shoulders did push work that morning |
| **Thu: Stair climber** | 5 min easy · 20 min moderately hard · 5 min easy (or a 30-min tempo run with the same structure) |
| **Fri: Swim, 100 m repeats** | 5 min easy · 8–10 × 100 m with ~20s rest · 5 min easy |

**Rope skipping progression:** add about 2 rounds a week, then lengthen the intervals, working up to 10 × 60s. Marching or jogging in place during a work interval is fine while your calves adapt.

## Floor sessions with your son (≈20–22 min, hands-free, pause any time)

Every floor session starts with the same **Desk Reset mobility block (≈10 min):**
cat-cow 60s · open book 2×45s (L/R) · thread the needle 2×30s (L/R) · 90/90 hip switches 60s · half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch 2×45s (L/R) · pigeon 2×45s (L/R) · down dog → cobra 45s · child's pose 60s.

It then continues with one of these:

**Core circuit (Sun / Tue / Thu), 2 rounds (≈11 min).** 40s work / 10s rest per station, and 45s rest between rounds:
dead bug · bird dog · glute bridge (he can sit on your hips) · side plank left (30s) · side plank right (30s) · plank · hollow body hold (30s).

**Flexibility (Mon / Wed / Fri, ≈11 min):**
standing forward fold 60s · seated hamstring 2×45s (L/R) · butterfly 60s · lying spinal twist 2×45s (L/R) · doorway chest stretch 2×45s (L/R) · couch stretch 2×45s (L/R) · upper-trap neck stretch 2×30s (L/R) · deep squat hold 60s.

## Progression and recovery

- **Weeks 1–2:** lighter weights (2–3 reps in reserve) and short rope intervals. Learn the movements and the morning routine.
- **Adding weight:** when you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add weight the next session (+2.5 kg, or +1–2 kg per dumbbell).
- **Every 5th week, go lighter:** same schedule, about half the sets, easy cardio.
- **Sleep:** lights out around **21:15** to get about 7.5 hours with a 4:45 alarm. This matters as much as the training.
- **Nutrition:** weight loss comes mostly from diet. Aim for a moderate calorie deficit and enough protein (roughly 1.6–2 g per kg of bodyweight per day) to keep muscle while you lose fat.
- **Warning signs:** if joints (not muscles) ache, or sleep and mood drop for more than a few days, take an extra easy day. Swap a run for a walk, or do the floor session only.
