# Workout Plan — Gal

**Goals:** a stronger back to counter slight postural kyphosis, plus core and glutes, without neglecting the rest of the body.
**Week:** two 50-minute gym sessions, a yoga class, a family walk, and a 5-minute posture routine every day.
**In the app:** everything below is loaded from `people/gal/plan/` in the data repo. The weights are conservative starting points, so adjust them in the first sessions.

| | Sun | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Main** | — | Gym A (50′) | — | Yoga class (50′) | Gym B (50′) | — | Family walk (45′) |
| **Daily** | Posture Reset (5′) | Posture Reset | Posture Reset | Posture Reset | Posture Reset | Posture Reset | Posture Reset |

- **Full body both days.** With two gym sessions a week, every muscle group gets trained twice. Gym A leans toward glutes and back; Gym B toward the posterior chain (hamstrings, glutes, lower back) and upper back.
- **Recovery:** Wednesday's yoga sits between the gym days, with 2–3 rest days around each gym session.
- **About two pulls for every push.** Rounded posture comes from strong, tight chest and front shoulders paired with a weak upper back, so pulling gets the extra volume.

## Gym sessions (≈50 min)

Each session: **5-min warm-up** (hands-free) → **≈35 min strength** → **8-min core circuit** (hands-free).

**Warm-up:** cat-cow 40s · open book 2×30s (L/R) · wall angels 40s · band pull-aparts 40s · glute bridge 40s · bodyweight squat 40s.

**How to read the tables:**
- **SS = superset:** one set of each exercise back to back, then rest. The app moves between the two automatically and rests after the pair.
- **Rep ranges (e.g. 8–12):** once she reaches the top of the range on every set, add weight. The app shows that hint.
- **Weights** are in kg, per dumbbell where noted. *BW* means bodyweight.

### Gym A — Glutes & Back (Monday)
| Exercise | Sets × reps | Start | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hip thrust | 3 × 8–12 | 20 (empty bar) | 1:15 |
| **SS** Goblet squat + Chest-supported dumbbell row | 3 × 8–10 / 10–12 | 8 / 6 per DB | 1:15 after the pair |
| **SS** Lat pulldown + Push-ups (hands on a bench to start) | 3 × 10–12 / 8–12 | 20 / BW | 1:00 after the pair |
| **SS** Face pulls + Hip abduction machine | 3 × 12–15 / 12–15 | 10 / 25 | 0:45 after the pair |
| **Core A**, 2 rounds | Dead bug 40s · Bird dog 40s · Side plank L 30s · Side plank R 30s · Plank 40s | — | 10s between stations, 45s between rounds |

### Gym B — Posterior Chain & Upper Back (Thursday)
| Exercise | Sets × reps | Start | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dumbbell Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8–10 | 8 per DB | 1:15 |
| **SS** Bulgarian split squat (per leg) + Seated cable row | 3 × 8–10 / 10–12 | BW / 20 | 1:15 after the pair |
| **SS** Seated dumbbell shoulder press + Incline prone Y-raise | 3 × 10–12 / 10–12 | 4 per DB / BW | 1:00 after the pair |
| **SS** Back extension (glute-focused) + Cable glute kickback (per leg) | 3 × 12–15 / 12–15 | BW / 5 | 0:45 after the pair |
| **Core B**, 2 rounds | Pallof press L 30s · Pallof press R 30s · Dead bug 40s · Glute-bridge march 40s · Plank 40s | — | 10s between stations, 45s between rounds |

**Why these exercises:**
- **Face pulls, prone Y-raises and rows** strengthen the upper back and the lower traps, the muscles that hold the shoulders back and down.
- **Supported rows** (chest-supported, seated cable) load the back without the lower back having to hold her up.
- **Core work is stability, not crunches** (dead bug, bird dog, planks, Pallof press). It builds a strong trunk without rounding the spine further.
- **Hip thrusts, Romanian deadlifts, split squats, abduction and kickbacks** train the glutes from several angles.

**Missing equipment:** each exercise's tips in the app name a substitute (bands for cables, floor glute bridges for hip thrusts, and so on).

## Posture Reset (daily, 5 min, no equipment)

This routine is identical in Gal's plan and Itay's. It needs only a wall, the floor, a chair and a doorway, and runs hands-free with 5s between moves:

| Move | Time | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Chin tucks (against a wall) | 40s | Head position — counters the forward head |
| Wall angels | 45s | Shoulder blades down and back, chest opening |
| Chair thoracic extension | 45s | Upper-back mobility (a foam roller works too) |
| Floor Y-raises | 40s | Lower traps |
| Shoulder blade squeezes | 40s | Upper-back endurance |
| Doorway chest stretch | 2 × 30s (L/R) | Tight chest and front shoulders |

**When:** any time. Mornings or after long sitting work best. On gym days it's short enough to do in addition to the session's warm-up.

## Progression
- **Weeks 1–2:** learn the movements with light weights, stopping each set with 2–3 reps still in the tank. Use this time to find real working weights.
- **Adding weight:** when she hits the top of the rep range on every set, add weight next session (+1–2 kg per dumbbell, +2.5–5 kg on machines).
- **Push-ups:** move from a bench toward the floor as she gets stronger. For split squats, add dumbbells once bodyweight gets easy.
- **Every 5th week, go lighter:** the same sessions with about half the sets.
- **If anything hurts:** the kyphosis is postural only, so none of this should cause pain. Pain (as opposed to muscle effort) means lighter weights, and a physiotherapist if it persists.
